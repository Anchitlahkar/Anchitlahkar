## ◈ RESEARCH

<div align="center">

> **Quantum-Inspired Optimization for Protein Folding**  
> Undergraduate Research · SRMIST · Under Dr. Manju A.

</div>

Pipeline built on **Qiskit** for quantum circuit construction and **PyRosetta** for energy scoring.

**Key areas:** Combinatorial optimization · Variational quantum algorithms · Energy landscape traversal · Hybrid classical-quantum pipelines
